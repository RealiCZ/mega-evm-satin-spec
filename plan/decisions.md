# Satin Specification — The Owner's Decisions

The owner's answers to the questions of [questions.md](questions.md), decided on 8 November 2026 (QG-07, and QH-01 and QH-02, raised by the review of the written section, on 9 November 2026), as the Satin section is written from them.
Each line gives the question, the decision, and the reason in one line; where a decision changes an implementation, the change is listed in [changes-evm.md](changes-evm.md) for the execution pages and in the block pages' own list for the rest.

## The rule for the QE items

Among the QE items, where the two implementations differ, Satin follows the behaviour of evm2 — implementation (b) and evm2 upstream — and implementation (a) changes where it differs.
This rule covers the QE items only; every other question follows its recommendation unless the table says otherwise.

## The Rex6-parity decisions (QI)

On 9 November 2026 every Satin rule on system contracts and on block handling was compared with Rex6's specification and code, and the owner decided, item by item, what Satin keeps of its own changes, what returns to Rex6, and which Rex6 defects Satin fixes.
These items arose from that comparison, and QI-19 to QI-28 from later checks of the published text against the implementations, not from [questions.md](questions.md), so they have no entry there; their implementation changes are listed in [changes-blocks.md](changes-blocks.md) and [changes-evm.md](changes-evm.md).

The rule they follow, as the owner set it: a Rex6 defect is fixed; a requirement Rex6 makes mandatory Satin does not relax; and Satin adds nothing of its own to the system contracts unless the addition is stated explicitly — refined the same day: Satin may change Rex6 where the change is more reasonable; a relaxation is unacceptable only when its side effects cannot be eliminated, and one whose side effects extra defences or operations can remove is brought to the owner with that analysis; every change is stated in the Satin pages as a change, with its reason, and every Rex6 defect Satin fixes is labelled `**Rex6 defect fixed.**` in the page's Rationale.

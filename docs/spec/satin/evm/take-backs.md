# Write and Take-Back Patterns

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Unstable** — Satin is under active development: every value and rule on this page may change before Satin is frozen.
{% endhint %}

Abstract: A transaction can make a write and take it back — write a slot and write it back, move value and move it back, create an account and destroy it, or fail the frame that wrote; this page states, for every such pattern, what each gas ledger and each resource count does, to which frame each give-back goes, and in what order, gathering in one place what the pages of the charges and counts specify.

## Motivation

A write that is taken back touches six figures at once: the regular gas of the opcodes and the EIP-3529 refund counter, the state gas of new state, the history gas of a [write record](../../glossary.md#write-record), and the data-size, KV-update and state-growth counts the [resource limits](resource-limits.md) hold.
Each figure has its own rule on its own page, and the rules differ in which frame they credit: a slot written back gives its gas to the frame that writes it back, a destruction gives to the destroying frame, a failed frame's rollback gives to the failed frame itself, and a refused or stopped start gives back to its caller.
Read one page at a time, the combination is easy to get wrong — a give-back credited to the frame that paid, a record taken back twice, a refund counted as a give-back — and any such slip changes a frame's `GAS`, its forwards and whether a later charge fits, which changes the transaction's path.

## Specification

The rules of this page apply to every transaction and every system call.
A system call pays no history gas, so every history charge and give-back below is zero for it (`SATIN-HIST-016`); a deposit and a system-originated transaction pay history gas and are given it back as any transaction is; a system-originated transaction and a system call are priced at `m = 1` and held to no limit, their counts still being made (`SATIN-STATE-009`, `SATIN-LIM-001`).
Each row of a table is a pattern of writes; the rules each row follows from are named in its first cell, and the row restates them for that pattern.
A table is one rule, and a row is cited as `SATIN-TAKE-<NNN>/<row>`, the row named by its first cell's label.

### The ledgers and the frame credited

- `SATIN-TAKE-001` For every pattern of the tables below, a node MUST charge, give back and count as the pattern's row states, in the order the row states, on each of these:
  - **regular gas and the EIP-3529 refund counter** — the regular charges of the opcodes ([Gas Schedule and Intrinsic Gas](gas-schedule.md)), and the refund counter the inherited `SSTORE` rules raise and lower, which is capped and applied only when the transaction settles (`SATIN-POOL-018`);
  - **state gas** — a state charge and its refill ([State Gas and SALT Pricing](state-gas.md)), at the bucket multiplier `m` fixed for the transaction (`SATIN-STATE-006`), a charge's legacy share (`SATIN-STATE-012`) being state gas that regular gas pays;
  - **history gas** — a history charge and its give-back ([History Gas and the History Allowance](history-gas.md));
  - **state growth**, **KV updates** and **data size** — the three counts of [Resource Accounting](resource-accounting.md), on the net of the frame named.
- `SATIN-TAKE-002` A give-back of state gas or history gas MUST credit the reservoir of the frame that makes it — the frame running the opcode that takes the record or the state back, or the caller of a start when that start returns — and MUST NOT credit its regular gas, whichever pool paid the charge (`SATIN-HIST-007`, `SATIN-HIST-012`).
  The destruction table's give-back goes into the destroying frame's reservoir (`SATIN-SD-005`, `SATIN-SD-007`); the rollback of a failed frame gives the failed frame's own charges back into that frame's reservoir (`SATIN-POOL-014`); and a give-back made once the transaction's own frame has returned — the first frame's start record (`SATIN-HIST-005`), the state gas charged for its recipient or created account (`SATIN-POOL-015`) — is made by the settlement, into the transaction's reservoir.
  A frame's regular gas therefore never rises within the frame but by the unused regular gas a child returns (`SATIN-POOL-012`): the part of a charge that regular gas paid stays spent, and its give-back is reservoir, which pays only state and history and returns to the sender at the settlement when unused.
  A frame can pay state and history, while it runs, with gas an ancestor paid for what the frame takes back, up to the price of those records and that state, but cannot compute with it; what it does not spend returns up the chain with its reservoir when it succeeds, and is undone by its rollback when it fails (`SATIN-TAKE-004`).
- `SATIN-TAKE-003` A give-back of state or history gas MUST NOT be an EIP-3529 refund: it is credited at once, to the frame of `SATIN-TAKE-002`, is not capped, and does not change the refund counter; the refund counter MUST change only by the inherited `SSTORE` rules, a frame's changes to it merging into its caller's when it succeeds and being discarded when it fails.
- `SATIN-TAKE-004` Every count a frame makes, a fall and a take-back included, MUST be on that frame's own net: it merges into the caller's net when the frame succeeds and is discarded when it fails, and a record the failed frame took back stands again (`SATIN-ACCT-011`, `SATIN-LIM-006`).
  A count made outside every frame — the body, the applied authorities' records, the caller a deposit creates, an admitted Oracle hint — is on the transaction's net and is not discarded by a frame's failure (`SATIN-LIM-005`).
  The rollback of a failed frame MUST undo the give-backs the frame received together with the charges it made, as `SATIN-POOL-014` defines it: on a revert or a stop, `reservoir = reservoir + net_charged`, the reservoir it holds when it fails — a failed child's returned reservoir included — plus the net of its and its successful children's state and history charges, and its regular gas is what it has left, none of those charges returning to it; on an exceptional halt, its reservoir becomes the reservoir it received and its regular gas is consumed.

In the tables, `m` is the multiplier of the bucket the state lands in, and a give-back "into its reservoir" credits the reservoir of the frame named and never its regular gas (`SATIN-TAKE-002`).
In the storage table, the first value of a pattern is the slot's original value in the transaction (`SATIN-SD-003`), and `x` and `y` stand for non-zero values with `x ≠ y`, so each value row matches only its own sequence of writes: the no-op row matches every write of the present value, and the other value rows apply to writes that change it.
The rows rolled-back, descendant, ancestor and refused are not value patterns: each says how a write of a value row is accounted when its frame fails, when another frame takes it back, or when a limit refuses its record, and applies together with the value row of that write.
Regular gas is stated for a warm access; a cold one adds the cold surcharge the schedule names.

### Storage slot

- `SATIN-TAKE-005` A storage slot written by `SSTORE` and taken back MUST be accounted as this table states, for a slot of an account that is not marked for deletion (a marked account's slot is the marked row of `SATIN-TAKE-007`), each `SSTORE` in the order: the `SSTORE` minimum-gas check, the regular charge with the refund counter's change and the fresh-slot state charge or refill, then the limits on the record in the order data size, KV updates, state growth (`SATIN-LIM-010`), then the history give-back of a record taken back, then the history charge of a record made (`SATIN-HIST-006`, `SATIN-HIST-013`):

| Pattern | Regular gas and EIP-3529 refund | State gas | History gas | State growth | KV updates | Data size | Frame credited | Order |
| ------- | ------------------------------- | --------- | ----------- | ------------ | ---------- | --------- | -------------- | ----- |
| no-op: an `SSTORE` that writes the slot's present value, `p → p`, in any pattern (`SATIN-ACCT-016`) | `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST`; the refund counter does not change | None | None | None | None | None | No give-back | The regular charge alone |
| zero-back: `0 → x → 0` (`SATIN-STATE-008`, `SATIN-ACCT-004`, `SATIN-ACCT-016`) | First write `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST`; second `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST` and refund `+SSTORE_RESTORE_REFUND` | `+SLOT_STATE_GAS × m` — its legacy share from regular gas, the rest from the reservoir first — then the refill `−SLOT_STATE_GAS × m`, into the reservoir | `+RECORD_HISTORY_GAS`, then `−RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` | +1, then −1 | +1, then −1 | `+WRITE_RECORD_SIZE`, then `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` | The frame running the second `SSTORE`, into its reservoir | Second `SSTORE`: its regular charge, refund and refill, then the limits, then the history give-back |
| restore: `x → y → x` (`SATIN-ACCT-004`, `SATIN-ACCT-016`) | First `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST`; second `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST` and refund `+SSTORE_RESTORE_REFUND` | None | `+RECORD_HISTORY_GAS`, then `−RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` | None | +1, then −1 | `+WRITE_RECORD_SIZE`, then `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` | The frame running the second `SSTORE`, into its reservoir | Second `SSTORE`: its regular charge and refund, then the limits, then the history give-back |
| clear-back: `x → 0 → x` (`SATIN-ACCT-004`, `SATIN-ACCT-016`) | First `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST` and refund `+SSTORE_CLEAR_REFUND`; second `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST` and refund `−SSTORE_CLEAR_REFUND + SSTORE_RESTORE_REFUND` | None | `+RECORD_HISTORY_GAS`, then `−RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` | None | +1, then −1 | `+WRITE_RECORD_SIZE`, then `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` | The frame running the second `SSTORE`, into its reservoir | Second `SSTORE`: its regular charge and refund, then the limits, then the history give-back |
| rewrite: `0 → x → y` (`SATIN-STATE-008`, `SATIN-ACCT-016`) | First `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST`; second `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST`, no refund | `+SLOT_STATE_GAS × m` once, its legacy share from regular gas first; the second write charges and refills nothing | `+RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` once | +1 once | +1 once | `+WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` once | No give-back | First `SSTORE`: its regular and state charges, then the limits, then the history charge |
| again: `0 → x → 0 → x` (`SATIN-STATE-008`, `SATIN-ACCT-016`) | `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST`, `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST` with refund `+SSTORE_RESTORE_REFUND`, `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST + SSTORE_WRITE_COST`; the refund stays | Charged with its legacy share, refilled into the reservoir, charged with its legacy share again | Charged, given back into the reservoir, charged | +1, −1, +1 | +1, −1, +1 | `WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` added, taken back, added | The frame running the second `SSTORE`, for its give-backs | Each `SSTORE` in the order of zero-back or rewrite |
| rolled-back: a write discarded by the revert or halt of the writing frame or of a frame containing it (`SATIN-POOL-014`, `SATIN-ACCT-011`) | The `SSTORE`'s regular gas stays spent; a revert returns the failing frame's unspent regular gas to its caller, a halt consumes it; the failing frame's refund-counter changes, those merged into it included, are discarded | Given back by the rollback into the failing frame's reservoir — on a revert or a stop by `reservoir = reservoir + net_charged` (`SATIN-POOL-014`), on a halt the reservoir it received, what regular gas paid being consumed with it | As state gas | Discarded with the frame | Discarded; a record the frame took back stands again | Discarded | The failing frame, by its rollback | At the failing frame's end |
| descendant: a write a frame made that another frame running on the same storage takes back — a descendant by `DELEGATECALL`, `CALLCODE` or a call back into the writing contract, or a frame started on that storage after the writer returned successfully (`SATIN-HIST-007`, `SATIN-TAKE-002`) | The descendant pays `WARM_STORAGE_READ_COST`; its refund change, that of the value row of its write (zero-back, restore or clear-back), is on its own counter, which merges into its caller's when it succeeds and reaches the writer only when every frame between them succeeds (`SATIN-TAKE-003`) | A fresh slot (zero original): the writer paid the charge and the descendant receives the refill; a non-zero original: none | The writer paid the charge; the descendant receives the give-back | A fresh slot: −1 on the descendant's net, which can be negative; a non-zero original: none | −1 on the descendant's net | `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` on the descendant's net | The descendant, into its reservoir; when it fails, its give-backs are discarded, the record stands again and the writer's charges stay paid | In the descendant's `SSTORE`, as zero-back; merged or discarded at its return |
| ancestor: a write a descendant made and merged by succeeding, taken back by an ancestor (`SATIN-HIST-007`) | The ancestor's `SSTORE` and refund, as the value row of its write | A fresh slot: the ancestor receives the refill; a non-zero original: none | The ancestor receives the give-back | A fresh slot: −1 on the ancestor's net; a non-zero original: none | −1 on the ancestor's net | `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` on the ancestor's net | The ancestor, into its reservoir | In the ancestor's `SSTORE`, as zero-back |
| refused: a write whose record crosses a limit (`SATIN-HIST-013`, `SATIN-STOP-004`) | The regular charge stays spent; the frame is stopped and reverts with the gas it has left | A fresh-slot charge the write made before the limits is given back by the stopped frame's rollback | Not charged | Not counted | Not counted | Not counted | The stopped frame, by its rollback | The regular and state charges, then the limit that crosses; no history |

  Example (descendant): in a transaction without a reservoir, a contract P whose slot 0 has the original value 7 and has not been written in the transaction writes 8, paying `RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` (3,520) for the record from its regular gas, then makes a `DELEGATECALL` to C, which receives no reservoir.
  C emits an empty `LOG0`, whose 2,816 of history gas C's regular gas pays, and writes slot 0 back to 7: the 3,520 go into C's reservoir, which held nothing, and C's regular gas does not rise — after the `SSTORE`'s own 100 it holds 100 less than before the write, and a `GAS` it runs next reads what it would without the give-back; C's refund counter rises by `SSTORE_RESTORE_REFUND`, its KV net is −1, and its data-size net is −8: −40 for the record taken back and +32 for the `LOG0`.
  C can pay later state and history charges from those 3,520 but cannot compute with them.
  When C returns successfully, its unspent regular gas returns to P, its 3,520 of reservoir join P's reservoir, and its nets merge: P's record is gone, the regular gas P and C spent stays spent, and the 3,520, unless a later charge uses them, return to the sender at the settlement.
  When C reverts instead, the slot is 8 again and P's record and its 3,520 stand; C's rollback (`SATIN-POOL-014`) makes C's reservoir `3,520 + (2,816 − 3,520) = 2,816` — here, with no failed child below C, the zero it received plus the `LOG0`'s charge its regular gas paid — which returns to P's reservoir, and C's unspent regular gas, which those 2,816 had already left, returns to P's regular gas.
  If P then reverts too, P's reservoir becomes `2,816 + 3,520 = 6,336`: the 2,816 C's rollback returned to it stay, and the 3,520 of P's own record join them (`SATIN-POOL-014`); had P halted instead, its reservoir would become the zero it received.

### Account

- `SATIN-TAKE-006` An account's nonce, balance or code written and taken back MUST be accounted as this table states, an account's record existing while it differs from what the transaction loaded (`SATIN-ACCT-004`); the sender, whose account is a fixed record of the body, makes none (`SATIN-ACCT-008`):

| Pattern | Regular gas and EIP-3529 refund | State gas | History gas | State growth | KV updates | Data size | Frame credited | Order |
| ------- | ------------------------------- | --------- | ----------- | ------------ | ---------- | --------- | -------------- | ----- |
| sent-back: value sent from P to Q and sent back from Q to P, neither empty under EIP-161 (`SATIN-ACCT-004`, `SATIN-ACCT-006`, `SATIN-HIST-009`) | Each call's regular charges, `VALUE_TRANSFER_COST` among them; no refund | None | Each start, judged against what the transaction loaded: its caller pays `RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` after the forward for each record the start makes — an account the transfer changes that did not differ yet, never the sender (`SATIN-ACCT-008`) or a marked account — and is given back the price of each record the start takes back, an account the transfer returns to what was loaded; for P and Q unchanged before the round trip the first start makes two and the second takes both back, and for P and Q whose balances an earlier transfer between them already changed the first takes two back and the second makes two | None | On each start's frame, +1 for each record it makes and −1 for each it takes back | On each start's frame, `+WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` for each record it makes, `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` for each it takes back, and `+TRANSFER_LOG_SIZE`: both transfer logs stay while both starts are kept, a failing start's log being discarded with its frame | The caller of each start that takes records back, into its reservoir, once that start's frame returned with its start kept; if that frame fails, the records stand again and nothing is given back | Each start: its regular charges, the forward, then the history of the records it makes; the give-back of the records it takes back at its return, after the reservoir join of `SATIN-POOL-012` |
| destruct-back: an account a `SELFDESTRUCT` returns to what the transaction loaded, of an account not created in the transaction (`SATIN-HIST-006`, `SATIN-SD-012`) | The `SELFDESTRUCT`'s regular charge; no refund | None | `RECORD_HISTORY_GAS` given back for each record the move takes back — each account it returns to what was loaded that had a record, the sender having none (`SATIN-ACCT-008`) — before the history of a record the move makes is charged | None | −1 for each record taken back | `−WRITE_RECORD_SIZE` for each record taken back, `+TRANSFER_LOG_SIZE` for the move | The frame running the `SELFDESTRUCT`, into its reservoir | `SATIN-SD-019`: its charges, then the records it takes back, then its positive effects held to the limits, then the history of the records it makes |
| failed-new: a value `CALL` to an account empty under EIP-161 that returns without that account added — such an account has no code, so this is a precompile at an empty address that fails, a start a limit stops, or a start refused after the charge (the precompile-failed, start-stopped, answer-stopped and refused rows of `SATIN-TAKE-010`) (`SATIN-HIST-010`, `SATIN-POOL-015`, `SATIN-HIST-014`) | The call's regular charges stay spent | `+ACCOUNT_STATE_GAS × m` charged to the caller before the forward, its 25,000-gas legacy share from regular gas first, given back into the caller's reservoir, share included, after the reservoir the child returns was joined (`SATIN-POOL-012`) | The start's records charged to the caller after the forward, given back to the caller when the call returns without the account; a refused start makes none and is charged none (`SATIN-HIST-015`) | +1 on the start, discarded; none for a refused start | The start's records, discarded; none for a refused start | The start's records and transfer log, discarded; none for a refused start | The caller, into its reservoir | `SATIN-HIST-010` at the call; at the return the reservoir join, then the state give-back, then the records' give-back |
| self: a value `CALLCODE`, or a value `CALL` to the calling account itself (`SATIN-ACCT-007`, `SATIN-LOG-003`) | The call's regular charges | None | None | None | None | None: no record and no transfer log | No give-back | None |
| keyless-back: a keyless deployment from signer nonce 1 whose creation's bump is set back (`SATIN-KEY-013`) | The call's regular charges | Step 10 charged nothing (the signer is not empty) | The history step 14 charged for the signer's record — none when the transaction had changed the signer's account already, the sender among such accounts — given back when the set-back leaves the signer as loaded | None | The signer's record: none when the set-back leaves the account as loaded | None for the signer's record | The keyless call, into its reservoir | At the creation's return, by the nonce rule of `SATIN-KEY-013`, before the call answers |

  Example (sent-back): P, a contract whose account the transaction has not changed, calls Q, unchanged too, with 1 wei, and Q calls P back with 1 wei; neither is the sender.
  The first call charges P 7,040 of history after its forward and counts two records and a transfer log on its start: KV +2, data size +240.
  The second start, made by Q, takes both records back: KV −2 and data size −80 + 160 on that start, and once P's re-entered frame returns successfully Q is given 7,040 into its reservoir; that reservoir joins P's when Q returns, while the regular gas P paid for the two records stays spent.
  Had P bumped its nonce by a `CREATE` earlier in the transaction, P's account would differ already: the first call would make Q's record alone (3,520, KV +1, data size +40 + 160), and the second would take back Q's alone, P's nonce keeping P's record.
  Had Q sent P 1 wei earlier in the transaction, making both records then, the signs would turn: the first call, returning both balances to what was loaded, would take both records back and give P 7,040 into its reservoir at its return, and the second would make them again, charging Q 7,040 before its start; Q with 5,000 of regular gas left after that call's forward and no reservoir runs out of gas there.

### Created account and the destruction table

- `SATIN-TAKE-007` An account created in the transaction and destroyed by its first effective `SELFDESTRUCT`, and a creation that fails, MUST be accounted as this table states, the destruction table's give-back going to the destroying frame whichever frame paid, and following `SATIN-SD-019`'s order: the opcode's charges, the table's give-back, the positive effects held to the limits, the history of the records the opcode makes:

## Rationale

**Why gather the patterns on one page?**
The rules that make a write and take it back are spread over the pages of the charges and counts they own, and each is right on its own; what an implementer has to get right is their combination, which no single page shows.
One table per kind of write, with a column per figure, makes every combination explicit and lets a fixture name the pattern it exercises.

**Why does a give-back go to the frame that makes it, and only into its reservoir?**
A give-back is made by an opcode, and only the frame running it can receive gas at that moment: the frame that paid may be suspended above it, or may already have returned; the destruction table differs because the parts it gives back may have been paid by frames that have all returned.
It goes into the reservoir and never into regular gas so that no pattern of this page raises a frame's `GAS`: a slot cleared after a failed call, or a frame that reverts after writing, logging or creating state, leaves the gas it spent spent, as Rex6 did, and the give-back pays later state and history or returns to the sender ([Two-Pool Gas](gas-model.md)).

**Why is a give-back not an EIP-3529 refund?**
A refund is capped at a fifth of the gas used and settles only at the end, so it cannot cancel the charge it answers; a give-back of state or history gas is the charge undone, and it has to be exact, or a slot written and cleared would cost or earn gas; landing in the reservoir, it is immediate for state and history and settles with the reservoir for everything else.

## Security Considerations

- If a node credited a give-back to regular gas, a frame's `GAS` would rise within the frame by gas spent on state and history, and a contract that records a failure after a failed call only when it still holds enough gas — a lock cleared after the call, a reverting intermediary in between — would complete its handler where Rex6 ran out of gas; if it credited the frame that paid instead of the frame that makes it, the frames' reservoirs would differ from every other node's, and so would whether a later charge fits in a reservoir or spills onto regular gas.
- What the rule leaves open, and accepts: a failed call's state and history charges stay in the reservoir its revert returns, so they can still pay the state a failure handler creates; a slot set and cleared without a reservoir keeps its charge out of regular gas until the transaction ends ([Two-Pool Gas](gas-model.md)).
- If a failed frame kept a give-back it received, a callee could take back a record its caller paid for, collect the gas and revert, so the record stands while the gas has been spent twice.
- If a give-back counted as an EIP-3529 refund, its cap would leave part of a charge unanswered, and a slot written and written back would cost more than leaving it alone.
- If a take-back were counted on the frame that made the write instead of the frame that undoes it, a failed undo would leave the record taken back, and a transaction could keep a write without its count.
- This page preserves the invariant that a pattern whose writes all end as they began leaves no state gas, no history gas, no state growth, no KV update and no record bytes on any ledger, its regular gas and the transfer logs it emitted excepted.
